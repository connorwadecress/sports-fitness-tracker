Pitchside

A clickable prototype of the hockey, injury and recovery tracker. Everything you log is saved in this browser, so play with it freely.

  1. Tap the + button and log a match. Set the score, then say how you feel.
  2. Turn on "Picked up a knock" while logging to see an injury start tracking.
  3. Log a physio or biokineticist session and pick the exercises they gave you.
  4. Open Rehab and tap an exercise to see the picture and log sets.
  5. Check the daily summary on Today. The suggestions change with what you log.
